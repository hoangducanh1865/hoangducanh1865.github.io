Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-25

  • rclone-mydisk accesses Google Drive files only in Google accounts that belong to the app's owner, and only to list, copy and synchronize them between those accounts and the owner's own computers.
  • No data is sent to any third party. Files and OAuth tokens stay on the owner's devices and in the owner's Google accounts.
  • The app does not collect analytics, advertising data or personal information from anyone else.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
